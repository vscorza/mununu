## §3 Engineering contributions

### §3.1 Paige-Tarjan adapted to multi-relation CLTS (EXP-0009)

Algorithm sketch, soundness argument citing Paige-Tarjan 1987 Thm 3.3 and Hopcroft 1971 uniqueness, complexity O(m log n), fixture descriptions, results table with 95% CIs.

### §3.2 Transposed-CSR modal pre-image (EXP-0014)

Skolem-paradigm preservation, witness-ordering invariant, complexity (O(state_count) with constant-factor improvement), evaluation against per-state baseline.

### §3.3 Chaotic fixpoint iteration with witness-rank stability (EXP-0013, EXP-0013-witness)

Tarski's theorem, chaotic iteration in Cousot-Cousot, witness-rank stability lemma (proved in §3.3.2 or appendix), feature-flag rollout strategy.

### §3.4 Parallel modal evaluation and BFS-frontier composition (EXP-0015, EXP-0016)

State-disjoint write argument, BFS layer-order invariance, scaling study with `RAYON_NUM_THREADS={1,2,4,8,16}` matrix, near-linear scaling claims with 95% CIs.

## §5 Threats to validity

- **Hardware specificity.** Numbers reported on a single dedicated runner; portability validated on Apple Silicon and x86_64 via cross-architecture replay.
- **Autovectorizer dependence.** D1 educational post explores when LLVM lowers `bitvec` ops to SIMD; some absolute numbers are LLVM-version-dependent.
- **Witness-extraction caveats.** B5 chaotic iteration is gated until EXP-0013-witness closes; numbers in §3.3 are with the flag on, audited.
- **Soundness boundaries.** Mununu's documented `// SOUNDNESS:` decisions (async over-approx for liveness, eval_expr unknown-variable fallback, guard-failure-as-block) are encoded as executable regression tests in `crates/mununu-core/tests/soundness/`. The paper is honest about which verdicts are conservative and where.

## §7 Conclusion + reproducibility statement

Reproducibility statement (hard requirement):

> All experiments are archived under `experiments/EXP-NNNN/` in the public mununu repository. Each archive carries the commit SHA, dev-container digest (`docker/Dockerfile.dev`), hardware fingerprint, and exact command. `make replay EXP=NNNN` replays each experiment end-to-end and diffs the result against the archived sample within the published 95% CI. Camera-ready PDF embeds a QR code linking to the archive index.

Roadmap: BDDs revisited (only as an alternative backend), IC3-style abstraction refinement, incremental composition.
