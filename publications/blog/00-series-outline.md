## Editorial conventions

- Default to public `publications/blog/`. Posts containing client/embargo data move to `mununu-private/blog/` via `--private` flag in the publish step.
- Replay box at top of every post:

  ```
  Reproduce in 5 minutes:
    git clone https://github.com/<...>/mununu.git
    cd mununu
    make replay EXP=NNNN
  ```

- Footer of every post:

  ```
  Provenance: experiments/EXP-NNNN-<slug>/
  Commit: <sha> (linked)
  Hardware: <one-line>
  Container: <digest>
  ```

- Figures are generated, not hand-drawn. The figure source is committed (`scripts/plot_speedup.py --exp NNNN --kind <speedup|memory|scaling>`); the rendered SVG/PNG is committed under `publications/blog/figures/EXP-NNNN/`.

- Every speedup claim cites a 95% CI computed via Kalibera-Jones bootstrap on the Criterion samples. No bare "X× faster" numbers.
