### Why §B4 fails at this scale

Two converging reasons:

1. **`BitVec::==` is already early-exit.** `bitvec` implements equality as a word-level scan that bails on the first differing word. Most fixpoint iterations don't converge — the equality returns false after word 1 or 2 in O(1) average for non-converged cases.
2. **The clone is small and the alternative is serial.** For state counts ≤1024, BitVecs are ≤128 bytes ≈ 2 cache lines. `clone_bitvec` is Vec allocation + memcpy — both hardware-vectorized. The replacement word loop with per-word branch (`if new != dst[i]`) is a serial dependency chain via `changed |= ...`. At small sizes, the branchful loop loses to branchless memcpy + early-exit compare.
