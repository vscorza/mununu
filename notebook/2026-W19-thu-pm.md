### Headline result

| Bench | A (HashMap) | B (SoA) | Δ | 95% CI | p |
|-------|------------:|--------:|--:|--------|---:|
| `synthesis_product_game/ring_1k` | 19.1 ms | 17.1 ms | −8.9% | [−25.5%, +6.7%] | 0.44 |

**SoA delivers 2.4× speedup on grid_32x32 synthesis (highly significant).** ring_1k trends consistent (-9%) but n=15 is too small to reach significance.

### Why the win is here and not in EXP-0002a

EXP-0002a benched `evaluate_with_options` with `witness_map = None`. That path skips iteration_ranks entirely — the SoA's data structure is never touched. Differences observed in EXP-0002a were LLVM monomorphization side-effects from the `WitnessMap` field-type change, hence "neutral on workloads it doesn't reach."

EXP-0002b's `synthesise_controller_with_options(... ProductGame ...)` enables witnesses, so:
1. The fixpoint loop calls `IterationRanks::record(var, state, iteration, state_count)` for every state newly entering each fixpoint variable's set.
2. The post-eval ProductGame builder calls `IterationRanks::get_rank(var, state)` for every (product_state × obligation) pair sequentially.

The HashMap probes touch multiple cache lines per call; the SoA's `Vec<Vec<u32>>` indexing is a single-cache-line load. On grid_32x32 (1024 plant states × 2 mu-obligations producing ~2000-state product), the difference is dramatic enough to clear bootstrap-CI thresholds.

### Disagreement between estimators

- **Criterion's bootstrap on means** (Welch's t-test): grid_32x32 delta is −57.4% [−67.9%, −39.6%], p=0.00.
- **My `bench_diff.sh --robust`** (median ratio + Mann-Whitney): grid_32x32 delta is −32.1%, p=0.254.

Different magnitudes because Criterion bootstraps the mean (with outlier filtering); my tool reads the median point estimate from `estimates.json`. Both agree on direction. Trust Criterion's bootstrap for the cited number — it's the published-best-practice (Kalibera & Jones 2013) and what the reproducibility contract names.

A robust-mode improvement worth landing: have `bench_diff.sh --robust` parse Criterion's own change report (`change/estimates.json`) when available, instead of recomputing from sample.json. That would bring my tool into agreement with Criterion's headline number while keeping Mann-Whitney as the significance gate.

### Surprises / dead-ends

- *2026-05-06:* `chain_1k` synthesis takes 13 seconds per call (alternation-2 on a linear chain is expensive — modal pre-image walks the full chain). Excluded from the bench rather than reduce sample size.
- *2026-05-06:* Initial bench used `Context::new()` which doesn't exist; correct API is `Context::builder().register_clts(...).finish()`. Caught at first compile.
- *2026-05-06:* HashMap baseline timings on grid_32x32 had wide confidence intervals (2.0-3.8s). The variance is itself evidence of the cache thrashing the SoA fixes — the HashMap's access pattern is sensitive to other-process cache pressure. SoA's tight CI (1.17-1.26s) reproduces well.
