\begin{frame}[fragile]{Controllable Labels}
  \lstinputlisting[style=ctxdsl, firstline=22, lastline=28]{../examples/02a_single_label.ctxdsl}

  \bigskip
  \begin{itemize}
    \item A \ctxdsl{controllable} block declares labels the \textbf{controller can choose} to fire.
    \item Labels \emph{not} listed here are \alert{uncontrollable} --- they come from the environment.
    \item This distinction is the foundation of \textbf{controller synthesis}: find a strategy that only uses controllable actions to guarantee a property.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}[fragile]{Labeled Modalities}
  \lstinputlisting[style=ctxdsl, firstline=70, lastline=74]{../examples/02a_single_label.ctxdsl}

  \bigskip
  The diamond modality can be \alert{filtered by label set}:

  \[
    \langle \text{labels} = \{ \texttt{cs\_assert} \} \rangle\; \textit{Selected}
  \]

  ``There \emph{exists} a transition labeled \texttt{cs\_assert} that leads to a state satisfying \textit{Selected}.''

  \medskip
  Compare with the unrestricted diamond $\Dia\varphi$ which considers \emph{all} outgoing transitions.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}[fragile]{Controller Synthesis}
  \lstinputlisting[style=ctxdsl, firstline=83, lastline=88]{../examples/02a_single_label.ctxdsl}

  \bigskip
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \ctxdsl{controller} names a synthesis task.
    \item \ctxdsl{source} selects the automaton.
    \item \ctxdsl{satisfying} references the property to enforce.
  \end{itemize}

  \medskip
  \textbf{Run synthesis:}
  \begin{shellcode}
$ mununu context synth 02a_single_label.ctxdsl \
    --controller spi_safety
Controller spi_safety: REALIZABLE
  \end{shellcode}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{New Syntax Summary}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabularx}{0.85\textwidth}{lX}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Keyword}      & \textbf{Purpose} \\
    \midrule
    \ctxdsl{controllable}  & Declares labels the controller may choose \\
    \ctxdsl{controllers}   & Block of controller synthesis tasks \\
    \ctxdsl{controller}    & One named synthesis task \\
    \ctxdsl{source}        & The automaton to synthesize a controller for \\
    \ctxdsl{satisfying}    & The property the controller must enforce \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  \textbf{Key idea:} Controllable vs.\ uncontrollable labels let us ask ``can we \emph{choose} actions to guarantee a property, regardless of what the environment does?''
\end{frame}
