\begin{frame}{The Concept: Simultaneous Signals}
  In hardware buses, \alert{multiple signals fire in a single clock cycle}.

  \medskip
  An AXI-like write transaction requires three signals \textbf{at once}:

  \begin{center}
  \begin{tikzpicture}[
    ->,>=Stealth,
    node distance=3cm,
    every state/.style={fill=ubaBlue!10, draw=ubaBlue, thick, minimum size=1cm, font=\footnotesize},
    every edge/.style={draw, thick},
  ]
    \node[state, initial]  (I)  {Idle};
    \node[state]           (W)  [right=of I]   {WritePhase};
    \node[state]           (R)  [below=of I]   {ReadPhase};
    \node[state]           (A)  [below right=1.5cm and 3cm of I] {Acknowledged};

    \path (I)  edge              node[above, align=center]
                {\tiny addr\_valid,\\[-2pt]\tiny data\_valid,\\[-2pt]\tiny ctrl\_write} (W)
          (I)  edge              node[left, align=center]
                {\tiny addr\_valid,\\[-2pt]\tiny ctrl\_read} (R)
          (I)  edge [loop above] node[above] {\tiny idle} ()
          (W)  edge              node[right] {\tiny ack} (A)
          (R)  edge              node[below] {\tiny ack} (A)
          (A)  edge [bend left=15] node[below] {\tiny idle} (I);
  \end{tikzpicture}
  \end{center}

  Write needs 3 labels; read needs 2. The \textbf{multi-label} syntax captures this naturally.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Multi-Label Syntax}

  \bigskip
  Line 40 is the key pattern:
  \begin{ctxdslcode}
transition Idle -> WritePhase
    on label addr_valid, label data_valid, label ctrl_write;
  \end{ctxdslcode}

  All three labels fire \textbf{simultaneously} on this single transition.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Single vs.\ Multi-Label Comparison}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabularx}{0.9\textwidth}{lXX}
    \toprule
    & \textbf{Single-Label} (02a) & \textbf{Multi-Label} (02b) \\
    \midrule
    Syntax & \ctxdsl{on label L} & \ctxdsl{on label L1, label L2, ...} \\
    \addlinespace
    Semantics & One event per transition & Multiple simultaneous events \\
    \addlinespace
    Use case & Sequential protocols (SPI) & Parallel buses (AXI) \\
    \addlinespace
    Label count & Exactly one & One or more \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  \textbf{Rule of thumb:} if your system has signals that must always appear together, model them as a single multi-label transition rather than chaining separate steps.
\end{frame}
