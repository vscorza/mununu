\begin{frame}[fragile]{An Impossible Specification}
  \lstinputlisting[style=ctxdsl, firstline=47, lastline=59]{../examples/09_unrealizable.ctxdsl}

  \bigskip
  \begin{description}
    \item[\ctxdsl{impossible_spec}:] \texttt{Open \&\& Closed} --- no state is simultaneously Open \emph{and} Closed. \alert{Unsatisfiable} (0/2 states).
    \item[\ctxdsl{open_reachable}:] $\lfp X.\;(\text{Open} \lor \Dia X)$ --- the valve \emph{can} reach Open. \textbf{Satisfiable} (2/2 states).
  \end{description}

  \medskip
  The impossible spec is not a syntactic error --- it is \emph{logically} vacuous.
  Mununu detects this during synthesis.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Formula Inversion}
  Inverting a formula computes the \textbf{environment's winning region} --- the set of states from which the environment can violate the specification.

  \bigskip
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Original} & \textbf{Inverted} & \textbf{Effect} \\
    \midrule
    $\lfp$ (mu)  & $\gfp$ (nu)  & Reachability $\leftrightarrow$ Safety \\
    $\gfp$ (nu)  & $\lfp$ (mu)  & Safety $\leftrightarrow$ Reachability \\
    $[\;]$ (box) & $\langle\;\rangle$ (diamond) & Universal $\leftrightarrow$ Existential \\
    $\langle\;\rangle$ (diamond) & $[\;]$ (box) & Existential $\leftrightarrow$ Universal \\
    \texttt{ctrl=controllable} & \texttt{ctrl=environment} & Controller $\leftrightarrow$ Environment \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  The inverted formula's satisfying states are exactly those where the \alert{environment wins}.

  \medskip
  {\scriptsize\color{ubaGray} Reference: Zielonka, W. (1998). ``Infinite Games on Finitely Coloured Graphs with Applications to Automata on Infinite Trees''. \emph{Theoretical Computer Science}.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Lasso Traces}
  A \textbf{lasso trace} is a counterexample for liveness properties. It has the form:

  \bigskip
  \begin{center}
    \texttt{prefix} $\;\to\;$ \texttt{(cycle)}$^\omega$
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  \textbf{Example} (traffic light that never reaches Green):

  \medskip
  \begin{center}
    \texttt{Red} $\;\to\;$ \texttt{(PedWaiting)}$^\omega$
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The \textbf{prefix} is a finite path from an initial state to the cycle entry.
    \item The \textbf{cycle} repeats forever, demonstrating that the goal is never reached.
    \item A counterexample for a liveness property ($\Globally\Finally\; p$) is an infinite path that \alert{avoids $p$ from some point onward}.
  \end{itemize}

  \medskip
  Mununu reports lasso traces when \ctxdsl{deadlock_traces} is enabled in diagnostics.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]{Counterstrategy Graph}
  When synthesis is unrealizable, Mununu can generate a \textbf{counterstrategy graph} --- a subgraph showing how the environment wins.

  \bigskip
  \textbf{CLI:}
  \begin{shellcode}
$ mununu context graph file.ctxdsl \
    --counterstrategy \
    --formula F --automaton A \
    --output cs.html
  \end{shellcode}

  \bigskip
  \textbf{UI:} Click the \textbf{``Counterstrategy''} button in the Verification tab.

  \bigskip
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The counterstrategy graph shows the \textbf{environment's winning region} --- states where the environment can force a violation.
    \item Edges represent the environment's optimal moves.
    \item Use it to understand \emph{which environment behaviors} make the spec unrealizable.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Takeaway}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabularx}{0.92\textwidth}{lX}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Concept} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
    \midrule
    Unrealizable & No controller can enforce the specification --- the property is too strong or contradictory. \\
    \ctxdsl{diagnostics}   & Enables detailed output explaining failure. \\
    \ctxdsl{counterexample} & Shows a concrete witness of why synthesis fails. \\
    \ctxdsl{deadlock_traces} & Shows traces leading to states where the controller gets stuck. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  \textbf{Best practices:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Always include a \textbf{realizable} formula for contrast (e.g., safety invariant).
    \item Enable diagnostics early --- they are your debugger for specifications.
    \item An unrealizable result is not a tool failure; it means the spec needs revision.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
