\begin{frame}[fragile]{An Impossible Specification}
  \lstinputlisting[style=ctxdsl, firstline=47, lastline=59]{../examples/09_unrealizable.ctxdsl}

  \bigskip
  \begin{description}
    \item[\ctxdsl{impossible_spec}:] \texttt{Open \&\& Closed} --- no state is simultaneously Open \emph{and} Closed. \alert{Unsatisfiable} (0/2 states).
    \item[\ctxdsl{open_reachable}:] $\lfp X.\;(\text{Open} \lor \Dia X)$ --- the valve \emph{can} reach Open. \textbf{Satisfiable} (2/2 states).
  \end{description}

  \medskip
  The impossible spec is not a syntactic error --- it is \emph{logically} vacuous.
  Mununu detects this during synthesis.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}[fragile]{Controller with Diagnostics}
  \lstinputlisting[style=ctxdsl, firstline=68, lastline=78]{../examples/09_unrealizable.ctxdsl}

  \bigskip
  Running synthesis with diagnostics enabled:

  \begin{shellcode}
$ mununu context synth 09_unrealizable.ctxdsl \
    --controller impossible_valve
Controller impossible_valve: UNREALIZABLE
  Violating initials: {Closed}
  Counterexample: no state satisfies (Open && Closed)
  Deadlock trace: Closed -[open_valve]-> Open (spec violated)
  \end{shellcode}

  \medskip
  The \ctxdsl{diagnostics} block enables \ctxdsl{counterexample} and \ctxdsl{deadlock_traces} --- they reveal \emph{why} no controller exists.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Takeaway}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabularx}{0.92\textwidth}{lX}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Concept} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
    \midrule
    Unrealizable & No controller can enforce the specification --- the property is too strong or contradictory. \\
    \ctxdsl{diagnostics}   & Enables detailed output explaining failure. \\
    \ctxdsl{counterexample} & Shows a concrete witness of why synthesis fails. \\
    \ctxdsl{deadlock_traces} & Shows traces leading to states where the controller gets stuck. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  \textbf{Best practices:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Always include a \textbf{realizable} formula for contrast (e.g., safety invariant).
    \item Enable diagnostics early --- they are your debugger for specifications.
    \item An unrealizable result is not a tool failure; it means the spec needs revision.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
