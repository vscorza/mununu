\begin{frame}{Deterministic Protocol}
  In a \textbf{deterministic} automaton, each (state, label) pair has \alert{exactly one} target:

  \bigskip
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tikzpicture}[
    ->,>=Stealth,
    node distance=2.8cm,
    every state/.style={fill=ubaBlue!10, draw=ubaBlue, thick, minimum size=1cm},
    every edge/.style={draw, thick},
  ]
    \node[state, initial]  (I)  {DetIdle};
    \node[state]           (P)  [right=of I]  {DetPending};
    \node[state]           (D)  [right=of P]  {DetDone};

    \path (I) edge              node[above] {\footnotesize det\_req}   (P)
          (P) edge              node[above] {\footnotesize det\_ack}   (D)
          (D) edge [bend left=30] node[below] {\footnotesize det\_reset} (I);
  \end{tikzpicture}
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  From \textbf{DetPending}, \texttt{det\_ack} leads to \emph{exactly one} state: \textbf{DetDone}.

  No ambiguity --- the next state is fully determined by the current state and the label.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Non-Deterministic Protocol}
  Now \texttt{nd\_ack} from \textbf{NdPending} leads to \alert{two different states}:

  \bigskip
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tikzpicture}[
    ->,>=Stealth,
    node distance=2.5cm,
    every state/.style={fill=ubaBlue!10, draw=ubaBlue, thick, minimum size=1cm},
    every edge/.style={draw, thick},
  ]
    \node[state, initial]  (I)  {NdIdle};
    \node[state]           (P)  [right=of I]   {NdPending};
    \node[state]           (D)  [above right=1cm and 2.5cm of P] {NdDone};
    \node[state]           (R)  [below right=1cm and 2.5cm of P] {NdRetry};

    \path (I) edge                node[above]       {\footnotesize nd\_req}   (P)
          (P) edge                node[above left]  {\footnotesize \alert{nd\_ack}}  (D)
          (P) edge                node[below left]  {\footnotesize \alert{nd\_ack}}  (R)
          (R) edge [bend right=20] node[below]      {\footnotesize nd\_retry} (P)
          (D) edge [bend left=40]  node[above]      {\footnotesize nd\_reset} (I);
  \end{tikzpicture}
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  The same label \texttt{nd\_ack} from the same state \textbf{NdPending} --- two possible outcomes.

  Non-determinism models \textbf{uncertainty}: the environment decides which branch is taken.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Impact on Verification: Box vs Diamond}
  Non-determinism changes the meaning of modalities:

  \bigskip
  \begin{description}
    \item[$\Boxx$ --- Box (universal):] ``\emph{All} successors satisfy $\varphi$''

    \medskip
    $[\text{labels} = \{ \texttt{nd\_ack} \}]\; \textit{NdDone}$ is \alert{FALSE} for NdPending

    because one \texttt{nd\_ack}-successor is NdRetry, not NdDone.

    \bigskip
    \item[$\Dia$ --- Diamond (existential):] ``\emph{Some} successor satisfies $\varphi$''

    \medskip
    $\langle \text{labels} = \{ \texttt{nd\_ack} \} \rangle\; \textit{NdDone}$ is \textbf{TRUE} for NdPending

    because one \texttt{nd\_ack}-successor \emph{is} NdDone.
  \end{description}

  \bigskip
  In a \textbf{deterministic} model, box and diamond over a label agree (at most one successor).
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Takeaway}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabularx}{0.88\textwidth}{lX}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Concept}  & \textbf{Meaning} \\
    \midrule
    Deterministic      & Each (state, label) has at most one target. Fully predictable. \\
    Non-deterministic  & Same (state, label) can have multiple targets. Models uncertainty. \\
    \midrule
    Box $[\;]\;\varphi$          & Universal --- \emph{all} successors satisfy $\varphi$. \\
    Diamond $\langle\;\rangle\;\varphi$ & Existential --- \emph{some} successor satisfies $\varphi$. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
  \end{center}

  \bigskip
  \textbf{Key insight:} Non-determinism is not a bug --- it models environments whose behavior
  we cannot predict. Choose box or diamond depending on whether you need \emph{guarantees for all}
  or \emph{existence of some} outcome.
\end{frame}
