\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fast domain:} ADC samples at high rate, pushes to buffer.
  \item \textbf{Slow domain:} Processor reads from buffer, stores results.
  \item \textbf{FIFO:} Shared labels enforce cross-domain synchronization.
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Key Takeaways}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Shared labels = synchronization barriers.} \\
        Two automata sharing a label must take that transition together.
  \item \textbf{Non-shared labels = independent steps.} \\
        \ctxdsl{adc_start} only involves ADC; others are unaffected.
  \item \textbf{Flat composition models hierarchy.} \\
        Without nested composition, shared labels partition the system
        into logical domains.
  \item \textbf{State space is the full product.} \\
        $36$ states for four small components --- cross-domain systems
        grow quickly.
\end{enumerate}

\end{frame}
